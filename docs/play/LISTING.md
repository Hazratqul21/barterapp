**Nomi (≤30):** MAB — barter maydoni

**Qisqa tavsif (≤80):**
Pulsiz savdo: texnika, chorva, hosil va tovarlarni o'zaro almashtiring.

**To'liq tavsif:**
MAB — O'zbekiston bo'ylab barter maydoni. Ortiqcha narsangizni joylang,
evaziga nima kerakligini yozing — MAB sizga mos almashuvni topib beradi.

• Texnika, transport, chorva, qishloq xo'jaligi mahsulotlari, qurilish
  mollari va elektronika
• «Mosliklar» — sizga kerakli narsani beradigan va sizdagi narsani
  izlayotgan odamlar avtomatik topiladi
• Kerak bo'lsa farqini pul bilan tenglashtirasiz
• Ilova ichidagi chat va takliflar
• Kelishuv bosqichlari: taklif → kelishildi → topshirildi → yakunlandi
• Ishonch bali, tasdiqlangan profillar va sharhlar
• O'zbek, rus va ingliz tillarida

Sotmang — almashtiring.

## English

**Title:** MAB — barter marketplace

**Short description:**
Trade without cash: swap machinery, livestock, harvest and goods directly.

**Full description:**
MAB is a barter marketplace across Uzbekistan. List what you have spare, say
what you want in return, and MAB finds a matching swap.

• Machinery, transport, livestock, farm produce, building materials and
  electronics
• "Matches" — people who have what you need and need what you have are found
  automatically
• Settle the difference in cash when the values don't line up
• In-app chat and offers
• Deal stages: offered → agreed → handed over → completed
• Trust score, verified profiles and reviews
• In Uzbek, Russian and English

Don't sell — swap.
